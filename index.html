What is Pivot Ladder?

Pivot Ladder is a free stock pivot point calculator for day traders. It uses the classic (floor) pivot point formula on the prior regular session's High, Low and Close, and lists the pivot point (PP), resistance levels R1–R4 and support levels S1–S4, with the midpoint between every pair of adjacent levels. Use it for stocks, ETFs and leveraged ETFs such as SOXL and SOXS.

How to use the pivot point calculator

1. Enter the ticker and the trading date. 2. Enter the prior session's High, Low and Close. 3. Read the support and resistance ladder, then copy the image or the values for your trading plan.
